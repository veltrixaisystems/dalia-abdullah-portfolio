A

Simulation-first architecture isolates perception, planning and control failures before hardware transfer.

B

Every autonomy decision should produce telemetry that can be inspected after a failure.

C

Safety and recovery remain explicit subsystems instead of being hidden inside one learned policy.

01

Navigation Reliability

Run repeated point-to-point navigation trials across static and dynamic maps.

Measuresuccess rate · collision rate · completion time
02

Localization Robustness

Stress SLAM/state estimation under sensor noise, occlusion and wheel-slip scenarios.

MeasureATE / RMSE · relocalization time · drift
03

Recovery Behavior

Inject blocked paths and local-planner failures to test recovery and replanning policies.

Measurerecovery rate · replans · intervention rate
04

Efficiency Trade-offs

Compare safer vs. faster planner/controller settings across identical mission sets.

Measurepath efficiency · latency · safety margin
